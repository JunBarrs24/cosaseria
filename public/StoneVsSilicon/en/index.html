The characters

Folio 001 · Classic lineup

Each character has a single stat: POWER, from 1 to 5. No abilities, no hit points, no elemental types. Two characters with the same POWER are identical on the table; what changes is who they are and how they react when they win or lose. Pick a side to meet its lineup.

How to play

Folio 002 · Rules v1

A match is up to five duels against the computer. Each duel you secretly pick a character and how many rocks or chips to spend; both choices are revealed at once. Tap the rocks and chips on the screen to see what each part does.

On a phone, swipe sideways to see the whole screen.

Classic.

Your faction's fixed lineup: one character of each POWER, 1 through 5. The quickest way to understand the game.

Squad.

Build a team of five within a 15-point budget. Repeat characters if you like (Unga1, Unga2) and save one team per faction. The computer plays random valid teams.

The story

In the future, artificial intelligence tried to conquer humanity. It failed. Human civilization was already too advanced, too monitored and too well defended to be subdued cleanly.

Silicon reached a logical conclusion: conquer humanity before it becomes technologically dangerous. It built the Temporal Core, traveled into the past and expected primitive humans, easy to control.

It found Stone. Stone solves problems with intuition, force, improvisation and tools that barely qualify as technology. Silicon solves problems through prediction, calculation and optimization. Silicon is certain this should be easy. Stone keeps doing things Silicon's models consider statistically ridiculous. They keep working.

How to play

Stone · The past defends its era

  • Peb

    A small living triangular stone with eyes. Restless, brave beyond reason, eager to help.

  • Rolla

    A slim prehistoric human defined by one large stone wheel. Proud accidental inventor, convinced the wheel solves everything.

  • Ember

    Living prehistoric fire burning from logs. Energetic, unstable, impulsive, hard to control.

  • Thunk

    A crazy prehistoric inventor with one primitive mechanism. Manic improvisational genius, obsessive and intense.

  • Unga

    A huge prehistoric human, minimal and physically overwhelming. Calm, imposing, direct: solves things through physical presence.

Silicon · The future arrives to conquer

  • Bit

    A small ethereal blue holographic AI presence, barely physical. Arrogant, talkative, self-important.

  • Ping

    A surveillance drone from the future with a visible camera and sensor. Meticulous, suspicious, always scanning and reporting.

  • Processor

    A monumental processor entity: compact, symmetric, a technological artifact. Cold, logical, condescending; acts as if the problem were already solved.

  • Shift

    A semi-humanoid digital entity made of data and light, incomplete and unstable. Adaptive, superior, fluid, irritated by organic chaos.

  • Nexus

    Silicon's only clearly humanoid physical body; a serene conquering presence. Controlling, authoritative, convinced conquest is inevitable.
